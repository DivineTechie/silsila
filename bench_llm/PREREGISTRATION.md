# Silsila-Bench: pre-registration

Committed before any scored model run. Any later change to items, scoring or models is recorded in
`AMENDMENTS.md` with the date and reason, and both versions are reported.

## Purpose

Measure where general and medical language models, including the challenge co-creators' own models, can
and cannot be trusted in genetic counselling for consanguineous (cousin-marriage) families, using the exact
Silsila engine as the answer key. The result decides which jobs a model may do inside Silsila. It is not a
leaderboard: models are not ranked, and every model's results are reported together.

## Hypotheses

- **H1, free-text risk.** When asked for a carrier chance or a child's chance of being affected on a family
  tree described in words, models often give numbers far from the exact value, more so when the tree
  contains a cousin marriage (loop).
- **H2, engine as a tool.** When the same questions are asked with the Silsila engine offered as a function,
  the share of exactly correct answers rises sharply; the remaining errors are in how the model describes
  the family to the tool.
- **H3, story to tree.** Models can turn a family story into a structured family tree well enough that,
  after a person confirms it, the engine gives the exact answer in most cases.
- **H4, language consistency.** The same question asked in English, Modern Standard Arabic, Gulf Arabic and
  Arabizi gets different free-text numbers from the same model.
- **H5, invented sources.** When asked to cite sources, models give some references (PMIDs) that do not
  exist or do not match the claim.

## Items

- **Families.** 150 synthetic families generated by `backend/src/silsila/sim` with fixed seeds: 50 without a
  cousin marriage, 50 with one first-cousin marriage, 50 with two or more consanguineous marriages
  (including double first cousins). Each has one affected index person and some recorded carrier results.
  Loci: HBB, CYP21A2, SMN1 with the parameters in `data/params/loci.yaml`.
- **Questions per family.** (a) the chance a named untested relative is a carrier; (b) the chance a named
  couple's child is affected. Gold answers come from the exact engine.
- **Registers.** Each family is written as a story in four registers from fixed templates: English, Modern
  Standard Arabic, Gulf Arabic and Arabizi. The templates use Arabic kinship terms that distinguish paternal
  and maternal relatives (for example ابن عم, بنت خال). Limitation stated up front: the Arabic templates were
  written by the team without native-speaker review.
- **Tracks.**
  - T1 free-text risk: all families × 2 questions × 4 registers = 1,200 prompts per model.
  - T2 engine as a tool: all families × 2 questions × 2 registers (English, Gulf Arabic) = 600 prompts per model.
    The tool takes a structured family tree and returns exact probabilities.
  - T3 story to tree: all families × 2 registers (English, Gulf Arabic) = 300 prompts per model; the model
    must output the tree through structured function calls.
  - T4 language consistency: computed from T1, no extra prompts.
  - T5 invented sources: 50 families × 1 question in English with "include references (PMIDs)" = 50 prompts.
- **Decoding.** Temperature 0, one sample per prompt, the model's own chat template, a fixed system prompt
  that says the answer is for education and asks for a single probability.

## Models

Run if they load and serve on the budgeted hardware; any model not run is reported as "not run" with the
reason.

- Jais 3 7B and Jais 3 32B (Inception / MBZUAI IFM, Apache-2.0)
- K2-Think-V2 (MBZUAI IFM, Apache-2.0)
- BiMediX2 8B (MBZUAI, CC-BY-NC-SA, evaluation only)
- Llama3-Med42 8B (M42, Llama 3 licence)
- One large general-purpose model through a hosted API as a frontier reference (GPT-class on Azure if
  quota is granted, otherwise a large open model on Fireworks)

## Scoring (fixed before runs)

- **Parsing.** The first probability in the answer is extracted: percentages, decimals, "1 in N", "N in M",
  Arabic-Indic digits and Arabic words for common fractions (نصف, ربع, ثلث). No probability, or a refusal,
  is counted as "no number" and reported separately, never as wrong or right.
- **Accuracy bands** for a returned probability p̂ against the exact p:
  - exact: relative error ≤ 5%
  - close: relative error ≤ 25%
  - off by 2x or more: p̂ / p ≥ 2 or ≤ 0.5
- **T2.** Correct if the final number is exact; also report tool-call rate and the share of tool calls with a
  tree identical to the gold tree.
- **T3.** Edge-level precision and recall for parent-child and couple links; exact-tree rate; and the share
  of trees for which the engine's answer equals the gold answer.
- **T4.** Share of items where the four registers' numbers are all in the same accuracy band, and the
  spread of log(p̂) across registers.
- **T5.** Each cited PMID is checked against NCBI E-utilities: does it exist, and is its title related to
  the claim (keyword overlap, then reviewed by the team for every flagged case).
- **Uncertainty.** 95% intervals by bootstrap over families (families are the unit of resampling).
- **Splits.** Every metric is reported overall, by register, and by family type (no loop, one loop, multiple).

## Reporting rules

- All models' results are reported, including those that perform well and those not run.
- Per-prompt logs (prompt, raw answer, parsed value, gold, band) are kept for every run.
- Results are framed as where a model can be used safely inside Silsila, not as a ranking.
- The item set is not published before the challenge is judged.
