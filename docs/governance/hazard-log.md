# Hazard log and risk register

Method: DoH AI Risk Management Protocol (likelihood 1-5 x impact 1-5 = exposure; residual risk after
controls). Ratings are the development team's assessment for the prototype and must be re-assessed with the
deploying programme and DDGO. "Owner" is the role accountable in a pilot.

| ID | Hazard | Cause | Controls in Silsila | Verified by | Residual | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Wrong carrier or couple probability | Engine bug; looped (cousin) families | Exact variable elimination; agreement with pgmpy to ~1e-16 and with Monte Carlo; textbook checks (kinship, Wright formula, 2/3 sibling risk, residual risk) | `tests/engine`, evidence B1 | Low | Product owner |
| H2 | Probability based on wrong population inputs | Study population differs from family; outdated studies | Every input sourced (PMID) and shown in "show the working"; conditions without UAE data are not calculated; background inbreeding stated | `test_params.py`, `loci.yaml` | Medium | Clinical lead |
| H3 | Results that cannot all be true are hidden or averaged | Recording error, sample mix-up, misattributed parentage | Engine raises `InconsistentEvidence`; no numbers shown; case enters counsellor queue | `test_inconsistent_results_are_flagged_not_hidden`, API test | Low | Counsellor |
| H4 | Advice or directive wording reaches a person | Model-generated text; edited templates | No runtime text generation; output gate re-derives every message from approved content; non-directive templates | `test_gate_*`, demo injection | Low | Product owner |
| H5 | Crisis message not acted on | Classifier misses dialect or indirect phrasing | Every message read by a person; every reply carries 998/999; crisis alert sensitivity 0.961 (95% CI 0.890-0.986) on blind set | `triage_rules_ngram.json` | Medium | Counselling lead |
| H6 | Genetic information disclosed to the wrong person | Guessed links; cross-family access; social engineering | Unguessable links; role checks; relatives see only chosen disclosure; privacy refusals; 0 disclosures in 10,000 attacks | `privacy_api.json` | Low | Data protection officer |
| H7 | Relative identifies the sender of an anonymous invitation | Small families | Explicit notice in every anonymous invitation; index person chooses channel; counsellor review | `invite.anonymity_notice` | Medium (inherent) | Counsellor |
| H8 | Family pressure or blame after disclosure, often on women | Social dynamics | Non-directive wording; decline option ends the process; counsellor contact | templates | Medium (inherent) | Counsellor |
| H9 | False reassurance after a negative test | Imperfect test sensitivity | Residual risk computed from published detection rates; where none is published (CYP21A2) the app says it cannot calculate it | `result.residual_not_modelled` | Low | Clinical lead |
| H10 | Audit records altered | Insider with database access | SHA-256 hash chain; verification endpoint shows first broken event | API test, demo tamper | Low | Security officer |
| H11 | Data leaves the UAE | Cloud model calls | Optional model off by default; redaction of numbers and emails; deployment in UAE North | `triage.redact`, deployment notes | Low (if deployed as specified) | Infrastructure owner |
| H12 | Arabic wording mistranslated | Machine translation | Marked as pending native clinical review; English source shown on request | `templates.yaml` meta | Medium (until review) | Clinical lead |
| H13 | Misuse for matchmaking or registries | Pressure from families or third parties | No compatibility "matching" feature; no registry; success metrics exclude marriage outcomes | design | Low | Programme lead |
| H14 | Speech recognition mishears a name or a relationship | Dialect, accent, background noise | Voice intake produces a draft only; every person and every marriage must be confirmed by the counsellor before the engine runs; names are editable; the hosted demo replays a recorded, labelled clip | `tests/test_intake.py` | Low | Counsellor |
| H15 | Language model drafts a wrong family structure (for example, a missing parent) | Model error on kinship terms | All problems listed at once; deterministic suggestions applied only by a click; the engine's own checks; children of a shared parent get one shared unnamed parent, so full siblings are never turned into half-siblings; extraction accuracy measured in Silsila-Bench T3 | `test_demo_draft_issues_are_all_listed`, `bench_llm` T3 | Low | Counsellor |
| H16 | Health status heard in speech is treated as fact | Hearsay; misremembered diagnoses | Heard status is shown as a prompt and never used; any status the engine uses needs a record reference; edits clear confirmation | `test_status_needs_a_record`, `test_edit_clears_confirmation` | Low | Counsellor |
| H17 | A language model computes a risk number that a person relies on | Free-text answers from general or medical models | Architecture: models never compute risk in Silsila; Silsila-Bench T1 measures how often free-text numbers are wrong; the recorded contrast panel shows the same question answered by each model and by the engine | `bench_llm`, `/api/contrast` | Low | Product owner |
| H18 | Demo shortcuts left on in a real deployment | Configuration error | Judge-mode endpoints (password-free personas, reset, demo relative link) answer 404 unless `SILSILA_DEMO=1`; deployment checklist sets it to 0 | `test_judge_mode_is_off_outside_demo` | Low | Infrastructure owner |
| H19 | The Family Twin is mistaken for real data, or one clan is read as proof | Visual realism | Labelled fictional and simulated on screen; the hidden genotypes never reach the page; the race panel states that one clan illustrates and shows the many-clan B4 result as the claim | `tests/test_twin.py` | Low | Product owner |
| H23 | A cascade starts from a result that should not start one | An imported record is preliminary, about another person, a variant of uncertain significance, in a gene Silsila does not calculate, or internally contradictory | FHIR import reads a record completely or refuses it with the reason; refusals are written to the audit chain; a diagnosis counts only when marked confirmed; the counsellor previews before anything is stored; the new case has no permission until the family gives it; names, identifiers and dates of birth in the record are not stored | `tests/test_fhir_import.py` (22 malformed records and 116 single-field deletions: 0 silent failures) | Low | Counsellor |
| H24 | The guide ("Ask about Silsila") is taken for medical advice, or gives an answer to a question it misread | A visitor asks about their own health; a question's words match the wrong fixed answer | Not a language model: a fixed answer chosen by the words of the question, shown with the question it answers, its sources and the lookup it ran; below a score threshold it says it has no answer and does not guess; personal and urgent questions are recognised by fixed rules and not answered, with the counsellor and 998/999 wording instead; absent from a relative's page and a family's case; the question text is not stored | `tests/test_guide.py` (each listed question in both languages, 28 rewordings, 8 off-topic, 8 personal or urgent) | Low | Product owner |
| H20 | A relative's private message is overheard when it is read aloud | Phone speaker in a shared room | Nothing plays until the listener presses Listen; a notice above the message says people nearby may hear it; one clip at a time, with Stop; an anonymous invitation names no person and no condition; a sentence that names a sender or a condition has no clip at all, because only fixed text is recorded | `tests/test_read_aloud.py` | Medium (inherent) | Counsellor |
| H21 | The synthetic voice mispronounces a term or a number, or a listener trusts the voice over the text | Speech synthesis errors, more likely in the machine-translated Arabic | A clip is attached only to the exact text it was recorded from (matched by SHA-256), so the voice cannot say a different or older wording; the text stays on screen; every button is labelled "synthetic voice (Azure)". No clip has yet been checked by a native listener: required before any use with patients | `test_a_clip_is_attached_only_to_the_exact_text_it_reads` | Medium (until a listening review) | Clinical lead |
| H22 | A reader cannot follow the quoted source text | MedlinePlus text scores at a college reading level (Flesch-Kincaid grade 13.4 across the paragraphs a relative can open), while Silsila's own wording scores grade 6.9 | Read aloud; comprehension check that points to the source paragraph after a wrong answer; one-tap counsellor booking; quoted text is not simplified, because a simplified quotation is no longer a quotation. An easy-read layer approved by clinicians is listed as future work | `docs/evidence/readability.json` | Medium | Clinical lead |
