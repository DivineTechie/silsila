# Pilot protocol (proposal)

Structured with the DECIDE-AI reporting items for early-stage clinical evaluation of decision-support AI
(Vasey et al., Nat Med 2022;28:924-933, PMID 35585198). A proposal for discussion with the programme; not approved.

**Setting.** One Abu Dhabi premarital/preconception screening centre and the genetics clinic it refers to,
plus families identified through newborn genetic screening.

**Who uses it.** Index persons or their parents (family app), relatives they choose to inform (invitation
links), genetic counsellors (console).

**Phases**
1. Weeks 1-4, shadow mode. Counsellors enter real families; Silsila computes probabilities and invitation
   order but nothing is sent. Counsellors record agreement with each probability, ordering and triage label.
   Supports the DoH conformity assessment required for High residual risk (Standard 3.3.1.2.2).
2. Weeks 5-12, live. Families choose whether and how to inform relatives; counsellors approve every message.

**Primary measures (taken from the challenge statement)**
- Comprehension: teach-back score of relatives and index persons, by language and literacy.
- Counselling uptake and time from index result to counselling.
- Time to appropriate referral and completed carrier testing among relatives who choose it.
- At-risk couples identified among already-married relatives.
- Privacy: unauthorised disclosures (target 0); relatives' reports of feeling pressured.
- Equity: all measures by language, sex, age band and literacy.

**Explicitly not measured as success.** Cancelled or avoided marriages, or any particular reproductive choice.

**Safety monitoring.** Weekly review of the audit log, escalations, blocked outputs and inconsistent
families. Stopping rule: any unauthorised disclosure or any unapproved text reaching a person pauses the
pilot pending review.

**Reporting.** Monthly report to the programme and DoH DDGO (Standard 5.1).
